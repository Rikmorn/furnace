**Epic 2 · Slice 2.2.5b Phase A ("Connector enclosure") then landed (2026-07-03):** every routed connector is now an **enclosed tube** — side walls + ceiling with portal-shaped open ends — with a per-edge `WorldEdge.enclosure?: "open"` style (guardrail-height walls, no ceiling; absent = tube), killing the 2.2.5a-gate "bare floor strip through void" seam look. The enclosure is built in `connect.ts` as **vertical-walled rings quantized along the climb** (`enclosureBoxes`, `RING_RISE 0.25 < CEIL_T 0.3` so adjacent ring ceilings always overlap — sealed by construction; pitched slabs were rejected because their end faces lean along-climb and can't end flush against a vertical door plane); floors are unchanged (corridor slab / pitched ramp slab / `stepBoxes`), all enclosure boxes are both meshes and cuboid colliders (tubes = walls you can't fall past; `open` = real guardrails), and end treatment follows portal kind — flush at `door` planes (box-room portals sit at wall-centre, so ends bury half a wall-thickness — sealed by construction), `SEAM_OVERLAP`-embedded into the rock at `tunnel-mouth`s. A new exported `connectorSection(from, to)` single-sources the cross-section (outer width = max width + 2·`SHOULDER`, headroom = max height) between `connect.ts` geometry and `layout.ts` clearance math; `clearanceBoxes` (now exported — the containment contract is unit-tested: enclosure ⊆ grown clearance) pads tops by `ENCLOSURE_TOP_PAD = CEIL_T + RING_RISE`, and `portalExemption`'s vertical reach rose to match (without it the grown clearance sliver above the old exemption top sits in solid rock at every cave mouth → false-rejects). Placement is **style-blind** (`world.test.ts` unchanged except one authorized floor-only-proxy repoint). `world.ts` marks the `upperA → landing` descent `open` so the gate exercises both styles. **Post-gate fix (the user fell through a hole):** stair floors never carried seam aprons — treads cover `[td/2, run+td/2]`, so the descending mirror left a 0.44 m full-doorway gap over void at the landing door; `floorBoxes` now emits flat **end aprons** extending `SEAM_OVERLAP` past both portals like every other floor, and the new PURE `tests/world-seams.test.ts` sweeps every edge's walk line (extended into the rooms, lateral fan, exact oriented-box tops + voxel columns) asserting **no capsule-scale unsupported disc** — empirically verified to catch the pre-fix hole (sub-capsule slivers tolerated: the oblique-join threshold wedge is `docs/backlog/dungeon/oblique-join-threshold-wedge.md`, folded into Phase B thresholds). No `@furnace/core` change.
