**Epic 2 · Slice 2.0 ("Player Physics") then landed:** Epic 1's hand-rolled game-side slide collision (`slideMove`) was retired for a **real Rapier capsule + kinematic character controller**. Core gained the physics mechanism — capsule shape, `kinematicPosition` body, `setBodyNextKinematicTranslation`, and a world-owned character controller (`createCharacterController`/`computeMovement`/`destroyCharacterController` tracked on `WorldSlot.controllers`; `applyImpulsesToDynamicBodies`/`characterMass` enable shoving dynamic bodies) — all thin wraps over the vendored `@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`. The dungeon owns policy: a physics world with static cuboid colliders from `LEVEL_BOXES`, a kinematic player capsule driven by the controller (gravity/slide/step-up/grounded; `FpController` emits a desired per-tick move + places the camera from the body, which now owns position, with vertical velocity via `gravityStep`), shovable dynamic props + immovable feature blocks, and an enlarged level (side branch + second chamber).
