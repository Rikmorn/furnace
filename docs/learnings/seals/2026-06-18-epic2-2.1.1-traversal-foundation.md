**Epic 2 · Slice 2.1.1 ("Traversal Foundation") then landed (2026-06-18):** the generated trimesh floor wasn't walkable — a Rapier capsule stalls on the mesh's internal-edge ghost collisions — so the dungeon now drives the player with a custom `CharacterMover` (collide-and-slide built on the new core `physics.castRay`/`castShape` primitives: a horizontal shapecast slide pass + a separate downward-ray gravity/ground pass that snaps to the *true* ground normal + explicit step-up) instead of Rapier's built-in KCC, which **stays available in core, unused, as a future option**. The Slice 2.1 box-floor chamber workaround was removed (the generated floor is now the real walking surface), and convex ramp/step gate fixtures were added. The custom controller also **restores shoving dynamic props** — via core `RayHit.body` + a standalone `shoveDynamicBodies` helper that velocity-pushes a hit dynamic body — and **smooths the camera eye-height** to soften the vertical jumps from steps / bumpy terrain. The custom controller's pure math (`clipVelocity`/`isWalkable`) is unit-tested and its slide/ground/step passes are `.gpu.test.ts`-covered; traversal feel stays a visual gate.
