**Epic 2 · Slice 2.2.5a ("World Graph & Collision-Aware Placement") then landed (2026-07-02):** the world is now a **graph the placer solves**, not hand-tuned world positions. `packages/dungeon/src/aabb.ts` adds the `Aabb` contract (`aabbOfBoxes`/`aabbUnion`/`aabbIntersects`/`aabbContains`/`aabbIntersection`/`transformAabb`); `region.ts`'s `RegionData` gains a required `bounds: Aabb` — the placer's piece-vs-piece broad-phase unit, which `connect.ts placePiece` now transforms (conservative yaw-rotate + translate) alongside meshes/colliders/connections/instances — and `RegionKind` gains `"authored"` (the pinned hand-built level, participating in placement as an obstacle rather than a theme). The placer, `packages/dungeon/src/layout.ts` `layoutWorld(graph, seed)`, is a deterministic collision-aware incremental graph embedding in the Ma-2014/Edgar shape: pinned nodes seat first and stay frozen, then a most-constrained-first order (most already-placed neighbours first, ties broken by BFS depth from the pins, then id) places the rest one at a time by sampling candidate seatings — `join`-mating the node's portal to its already-placed parent portal at a seeded grid of connector lengths × yaw offsets (0°, ±15°, ±30°, ±45°) — hard-accepting the first candidate that clears the occupancy rules AND makes every now-both-placed edge (its own tree-seating edge, plus any loop-closing edges to already-placed neighbours) realizable via `route`, with bounded backtracking (a stack of revisable frames, a total-attempt cap) before throwing setup-loud with a per-node candidate-failure histogram. **Force-directed relaxation was research-overturned** as the placement foundation — no shipped system was found embedding a world graph by global force-directed relaxation; see `docs/research/2026-07-01-dungeon-2.2.5-layout-placement-and-megastructure.md` — in favour of this deterministic incremental embedder. Loop-closing edges never drive the candidate search that picks a node's position; they are only checked (and can trigger backtracking) once the tree edge has already placed the node. `layoutWorld` is pure: no GPU, no Rapier, no wall-clock. The collision model, `packages/dungeon/src/occupancy.ts`, tracks three obstacle classes — placed-piece **envelopes** (must never interpenetrate each other; exact touch is legal), placed-piece **solids** (conservative cuboid AABBs, or — for cave voxel proxies — exact per-cell membership, since the whole-grid AABB is mostly air and would false-reject any cave-mouth connector), and committed-connector **clearance volumes** (reserved walking air, segmented along a climb rather than one giant box) — checked pairwise across 5 acceptance rules (piece-vs-piece envelopes; piece envelopes vs. committed clearance air; clearance air vs. piece solids outside a **portal exemption** — a box around each portal a connector is allowed to bore through its own endpoints' walls near the seam, so only deeper clips reject; clearance air vs. a non-endpoint piece's envelope; and clearance air vs. another committed connector's air or its floor slab). `connect.ts route` was hardened for the new direction-agnostic caller: descending stair-runs now emit a real mirrored staircase instead of a `dh<0` empty step loop, and the ramp slope-limit guard now tests `Math.abs(pitch)` so a forced steep DESCENDING ramp throws setup-loud like its ascending counterpart — resolving the carried `connect-route-descending-stairs-empty` backlog. `themes/box-room.ts` generalizes `BoxRoomParams.door` (one `DoorSpec`) to `doors: DoorSpec[]` (≥1, at most one per cardinal side, setup-loud on violation) so rooms of degree > 1 exist for a graph world; `pillarHall` exposes this as an optional param (default = the historical single S door), `greatHall` stays single-door. The world-graph contract, `packages/dungeon/src/world-graph.ts`: nodes are region instances (a local-frame `RegionData` + optional frozen `pinned` placement), edges are connections between two node portals — verticality (`heightDelta`) and the reserved-for-later `directed`/`requiredVerbs` fields live on EDGES, not nodes; `validateGraph` throws setup-loud on dangling/duplicate/double-used portals, on any `directed`/non-`"walk"` edge (no fall verbs yet), and on a disconnected graph — connectivity means reachable-from-pins by BFS (a lone pinned obstacle with no edges is legally its own root). The proof graph, `packages/dungeon/src/world.ts` `buildWorldGraph`, pins the authored level as a **collision phantom** — empty meshes/instances but real `LEVEL_BOXES` colliders + bounds, so the placer treats it as a first-class obstacle while `main.ts` still realizes its visuals itself — plus a branching cave wing, two ground halls off the cave's east/north mouths (resolved by facing, not index), two elevated `pillarHall`s (one up a ramp, one up forced stairs, height-stacked to fit the same footprint), and a two-hop descending cycle: `upperA → landing` (a forced-stairs descent) then `landing → hallA` (a flat closing corridor), where `landing` is a new small room seated in the open void east of the authored level's wall (its stair-seat door is built full-height to avoid the descending connector's headroom clipping a normal lintel — the general case is tracked by the new `descending-connector-lintel-clip` backlog). **Design history, recorded honestly:** the original spec called for a direct `upperA ↔ hallA` descending loop; that proved geometrically infeasible during execution — a straight descending connector between the chamber-elevated `upperA` and the cave-seated `hallA` would have to cross the authored east wall / cave geometry and mate two far-apart, mis-angled doors — so it was redesigned (user-approved) to route both hops through the void `landing` room instead, keeping both hops clear of the authored wall while still exercising the descending-stairs path end-to-end. `compose.ts` is **deleted entirely** (`buildArea`/`attachWing`/`attachUpperRoom`/`attachUpperLevel`/`placeRoom` and all its GATE-TUNE world positions); `main.ts` now renders `layoutWorld(buildWorldGraph(WORLD_SEED))` directly — no hand-tuned world positions for the graph-placed content (the pre-existing baked cavern, seated at its authored `[0,0,-24]` outside the world graph, is unchanged). **No `@furnace/core` change.** Headless: `aabb`/`occupancy`/`world-graph`/`layout`/`box-room`/`connect` unit tests (incl. layout backtracking + an impossible-graph diagnostic-throw case) and `world.test.ts` (zero envelope overlap, determinism, the descending hop's >4 m drop, the closing hop's flat portal-Y check); GPU walks in `area-traversal.gpu.test.ts` (the cave→room seam, the ramp climb to `upperA`, the forced-stairs climb to `upperB`, and the ~10 m loop descent from `upperA` to `landing`) and the re-anchored `traversal.gpu.test.ts` (cave-hub fuzz, level↔wing seam, no-fall-through). Placement/theme feel stays a visual gate (Safari/Chrome). Open follow-ups: two backlog entries surfaced during execution (`docs/backlog/dungeon/descending-connector-lintel-clip.md`, `docs/backlog/dungeon/occupancy-piece-vs-connector-slab.md`); `docs/backlog/dungeon/connector-geometry-stitching.md` (untouched — surface stitching remains a separate gap); force-directed relaxation as a possible future *aesthetic* pass over an already-valid placement; and Slice 2.2.5b, which replaces this file's hand-authored topology with a generated graph.
