# Built interfaces at heterogeneous seams — research for Slice 2.2.5b Phase B1

> **Status:** Pre-decision research feeding the Slice 2.2.5b **B1 ("Built Interfaces")**
> spec, produced 2026-07-03 by a 3-agent cited prior-art pass (organic↔built transition
> pieces · stair/ramp landings · doorway thresholds). It grounded the three load-bearing
> B1 choices — the cave mouth **collar** (piece-side, masking not blending), the flat
> **low-end landing** on climbing connectors (the descending-lintel-clip fix), and the
> connector-emitted door-aligned **threshold plate** (the oblique-wedge fix) — confirming
> all three against shipped systems and building codes, and contributing two refinements
> (threshold lip; landing-length code bracket). The as-built design lives in `AGENTS.md`
> + source on landing.

**Epistemic key:** `[V]` verified from a cited source · `[K]` established general knowledge ·
`[I]` inference about fit to our pipeline.

Grounded in the dungeon's actual pipeline: SDF caves (Surface-Nets meshes, ~3.2 m bores
±0.5 m noise) present raw `tunnel-mouth` portals that Phase A's enclosed box connectors
embed straight into — the "boxy tube into raw isosurface" look the 2.2.5b-A visual gate
flagged. B1's doctrine: organic pieces grow built structures so every seam collapses to
the proven built↔built case.

---

## 1. Organic↔built transitions — masking with a built piece IS the professional pattern

- **[V]** Skyrim ships literal kit-to-kit transition assets including built→cave: the
  Creation Kit tutorial names `NorHallBg1wayToCaveG01` — a Nordic-hall kit piece whose far
  profile mates the CaveG cave kit — as a piece that "exist[s] specifically for this
  purpose". Piece-side ownership, and the *built* vocabulary owns the transition.
- **[V]** Burgess & Purkeypile: uniform doorway dimensions are the inter-kit contract
  ("determine a uniform dimension for door frames… transition from kit to kit without
  unique pieces"); caves are the named hard case ("highly freeform and organic" vs
  "rules-based and orthogonal" kits); off-grid joins got dedicated covering pieces (the
  Ratway archway "that follows the full curvature of the walls, ceiling and floor").
  Burgess elevates "transition metrics" to a foundational kit decision (80.lv interview).
- **[V]** Warframe (Game AI Pro ch. 7, Brewer): designer-crafted blocks expose typed
  portal *sockets* ("only compatible portals of the same size can be connected", "one or
  two portal types per procedural template"); after layout, a post-pass places **caps** on
  unused portals and **door fittings** on connected ones, "covering up any seams".
- **[V]** DunGen: doorway prefabs are the join-dressing mechanism — *Connectors* ("door
  frames… visual connection flourishes") spawn on successful connection, *Blockers*
  ("walls, closed doors, rubble piles") seal unused doorways.
- **[V]** Mining engineering supplies the vocabulary and the structural logic: a **portal**
  is "the structure surrounding the immediate entrance to a mine; the mouth of an adit or
  tunnel" and a **collar** is "the timbering or concrete around the mouth… of a shaft"
  (PA DEP mining glossary). An adit's entrance is shored and extended outward *because the
  rock face at the opening is the irregular, unreliable part* — 1:1 with our masking
  rationale. **[I]** No engineering citation found for framing-to-bore proportions — our
  collar depth/oversize (~1.2 m deep, envelope + margin) are aesthetic choices, GATE-TUNE.
- **[V]** Counter-evidence hunt (blending instead of masking): every shipped *blender*
  operates in a single representation — Deep Rock Galactic carves rooms and tunnels from
  one destructible medium ("pearls on a string", ~119 hand-crafted rooms unioned into the
  terrain); SDF middleware (Godot Voxel Tools) smooth-booleans caves into terrain *fields*.
  **No system found that CSG-blends mesh-kit geometry into an isosurface at runtime**, and
  no writeup found arguing transition pieces read cheap — masking + clutter is uniformly
  treated as the professional technique. **[K]** Raw unmasked interpenetration is what
  reads cheap (the No Man's Sky buildings-in-terrain complaint).
- **[I]** For us: piece-side collar (Skyrim's pattern) composes with a later
  Warframe-style generator-placed fitting if door↔door joins ever need extra dressing —
  no redesign. And Warframe caps ≙ DunGen blockers: when the B2 generator leaves an
  unused cave mouth, precedent says seal it with a built **cap** piece — trivial once the
  collar exists. The SDF-stamp option (union a box field into the cave field at the mouth)
  remains the honest path to true rim *blending* — Epic-3 stitching territory, not B1.

## 2. Flat low-end landings — building code, verbatim

- **[V]** IBC §1011.6: floor or landing required "at the top and bottom of each stairway",
  depth ≥ min(stair width, 1.22 m). IRC §R311.7.6: ≥ 0.91 m, top-of-flight exception
  exists but **the bottom landing is never waived**. OSHA 1910.25: platforms ≥ 0.76 m
  deep, and "when a door or gate opens directly on a stairway, a platform is provided" —
  the door-at-a-stair rule is literally *insert a flat platform*, our fix verbatim.
- **[V]** Ramps: ADA §405.7 landings at top AND bottom of every run, length ≥ 1.52 m;
  California CBC §1122A makes **bottom** ramp landings the longest (1.83 m vs 1.52 m top)
  — real-world precedent that the *lower* end of a slope needs the extra flat run.
- **[I]** Bracket for our constant: `LANDING_LEN = 1.5 m` exceeds all stair-landing minima
  and ≈ the ADA ramp minimum. Code-plausible for both connector kinds.
- **[V]** IBC §1011.3 headroom is a **sloped prism following the nosing line**, carried
  "one tread depth beyond the bottom riser" onto the landing; the bottom-of-flight pinch
  (low header/doorway at a stair exit) is a documented real-construction failure mode
  (InspectApedia) — our lintel-clip bug in building terms. Codes tolerate ≤ ~2 in of
  door-hardware intrusion (§1003.3.1), nothing lintel-sized.
- **[V]** Game prior art converges: VAZGRIZ mandates flat hallway at both stair ends +
  reserved headroom cells; DunGen and Minecraft jigsaw bake level door planes into
  authored vertical pieces (the built-interface doctrine applied to stairs); Dungeon
  Architect guarantees clearance by lattice quantization. Classic roguelikes degenerate
  stairs to teleporting tiles. **[I]** No system found generalizing "taller doorway at the
  stair foot" (our retired `LANDING_SEAT_DOOR` hack) — codes go the other way.
- **[V/I]** Credible alternative recorded: since code headroom follows the flight, the
  connector's clearance/ceiling *top* could pitch with the walk line (the basement-stair
  soffit) — shrinks the clip but the flat bottom landing is still mandatory, so it's a
  possible future refinement, not a replacement.

## 3. Door-aligned threshold plates — sized to the wall, owned by one emitter

- **[V]** Real thresholds/saddles bridge floor discontinuities at the door line and are
  sized `[door width (jamb-to-jamb)] × [wall thickness]` centered on the wall plane
  (stone thresholds conventionally 4.5 in wide "because modern walls are 4.5 in thick").
  ADA §404.2.5 caps threshold height at ½ in beveled — thresholds are *proud of the
  floor*, not flush. **[I]** Adopted refinement: emit the plate with a small lip above
  portal floor level (~0.01–0.02 m) — matches the real profile AND kills coplanar
  z-fighting, a documented modular-kit failure mode (coincident overlapping kit floors).
- **[V]** Ownership precedent: Warframe's generator emits one door fitting per connected
  portal pair (single owner, neither block); DunGen lets *both* doorways offer a connector
  prefab and must resolve with priority + random tiebreak — arbitration cost that argues
  for our single-emitter (the connector) design. Skyrim's oblique joins were patched with
  join-aligned covering pieces placed by hand.
- **[V]** Honest nuance: real interior doorways only get saddles where a discontinuity
  exists; our oblique joins always create one, so unconditional per-join emission is
  justified (and uniform is cheaper than conditional).
- **[V]** Oblique joins themselves: cardinal-only is the dominant middleware vocabulary
  (Edgar templates are integer-grid orthogonal polygons; DunGen rotates tiles 90°/180°/270°
  only). Oblique portal-mating IS shipped — Warframe rotates blocks "as necessary to line
  up compatible portals"; Dungeon Architect 2.30.0 (2023) added any-orientation doors on
  custom polygon bounds — but **nobody documents their oblique floor patch** (null
  result). Our wedge is exactly that undocumented cost; the plate is the code-shaped
  answer.

## Net assessment — design deltas adopted into the B1 spec

1. **Collar confirmed** (piece-side, masking): the only shipped organic↔built-specific
   transition (Skyrim) is piece-owned; blending is single-representation-only; mining's
   portal/collar gives the idiom and the names. Depth/oversize stay GATE-TUNE aesthetics.
2. **Landing confirmed** at 1.5 m (code bracket 0.76–1.83 m, bottom never waived);
   pitched-soffit clearance top recorded as a possible later refinement.
3. **Threshold plate confirmed**, one delta: plate top gets a ~0.015 m lip above portal
   floor level (real profile + z-fighting kill), still far under `STEP_HEIGHT` 0.4.
4. **B2 seam noted:** unused generated mouths should be sealed with built **caps**
   (Warframe/DunGen precedent) — trivial once the collar kit exists.
