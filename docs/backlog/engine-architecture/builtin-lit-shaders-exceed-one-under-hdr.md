# Built-in lit shaders exceed 1.0 under HDR (not exposure-calibrated)

The built-in `lit` and `texturedLit` shaders compute `albedo * (directional + hemi)` where `directional = LIGHT_COLOR * halfLambert²` (peaks ~1.0) and `hemi = mix(GROUND_COLOR, SKY_COLOR, …)` (adds ~0.55–0.72 on top) — see `packages/core/src/shader/builtins.ts` (`LIT_WGSL`, `TEXTURED_LIT_WGSL`). For an up-facing white surface lit by the key light the two terms SUM to ~1.4–1.5× albedo, so a white texel lands at ~1.5 in the render target — the lighting is not energy-conserving or exposure-calibrated (ambient stacks on top of full directional).

In the **LDR** path (pre-Stage-2) this was harmless: `bgra8unorm` clamped the ~1.5 to white. The **HDR** path (Stage 2b) preserves it, which surfaced two consequences in the bowling demo's Safari gate (2026-06-06):
1. **Thresholdless bloom haloed the lit lane**, not just the emissive ball — because the lit white checkerboard texels (~1.5) are genuine bloom sources, not 1.0. (Worked around demo-side: the bowling + cookbook demos now pass a soft-knee `threshold` above the lit-content ceiling, ~2.0 / ~1.3 respectively, to isolate the emissive elements. See `packages/hello-world/src/demos/bowling/scene.ts` `BLOOM_THRESHOLD`.)
2. **The whole lit scene is "hot"** — tonemap (PBR Neutral) compresses lit whites from ~1.5 → ~0.9, so lit surfaces read slightly desaturated/compressed rather than at their intended brightness, because there is no exposure calibration tying the built-in lighting to the tonemap's input range.

This is a **Stage-3 (lighting) concern**, not a post-processing bug — the bloom and tonemap behave correctly given the input. The fix belongs with proper lighting: make the built-in lighting HDR/tonemap-aware — normalize so a white surface under the key light sits at a deliberate exposure (e.g. key light ≈ 1.0, ambient a small fraction, not another full ~0.6 stacked on top), and decide the scene's target middle-grey/exposure relative to the tonemap. Until then, HDR consumers must threshold bloom above the lit-content ceiling and accept the compressed lit whites.

**Trigger to revisit:** Stage 3 (lighting epic) — when the built-in lit shaders are reworked for real lighting, calibrate their output range to the HDR/tonemap pipeline. Until then, demos work around it with a bloom threshold.

**Reference:** `packages/core/src/shader/builtins.ts` (`LIT_WGSL` / `TEXTURED_LIT_WGSL` — the `directional + hemi` sum); `packages/core/src/post/bloom.ts` (the soft-knee `threshold`/`softness` knobs that work around it); `packages/hello-world/src/demos/bowling/scene.ts` (`BLOOM_THRESHOLD = 2.0`); the Visual Fidelity epic Stage 3.
